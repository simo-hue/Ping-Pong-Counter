Argued about who was serving? Forgot if the score was 8-7 or 9-6? Had the rhythm of a perfect Table Tennis match ruined by simple scorekeeping errors? 

We have all been there. Ping Pong Scoreboard is the premium, ultimate companion app for iPhone and Apple Watch designed to solve this exact problem forever. Crafted with high-end, cyberpunk-inspired dark aesthetics, glowing neon scoring fields, and modern Apple APIs, it turns scorekeeping into a fluid, responsive, and gorgeous part of the sport.

Leave your iPhone on a tripod at the net or on the table, and keep score directly from your wrist. With instant synchronization, Live Activities, and a high-quality voice synthesizer announcing scores like a real referee, you can focus 100% on your game.

CORE FEATURES

1. Full-Screen Neon Scoreboard (OLED Optimized)
• Adaptive Layouts: Supports both horizontal (split-screen left/right for tabletop use at the net) and vertical layouts (split-screen top/bottom for quick one-handed use).
• Zero-Interference Gestures: Tap anywhere on a player's field to register a point (+1) with a dynamic floating neon animation.
• Swipe Down to Undo (-1): Made a mistake? Swipe down on the player's side to reverse the last action. Holds up to 30 steps of rollback history.
• Active Serve Pulse: A continuous glowing pulse indicates the serving side, so you always know who has the ball.
• Custom Player Names: Click the edit icon to personalize player names directly on the scoreboard.

2. Native Apple Watch Companion App
• True Hands-Free Scoring: Leave your phone at the side of the table and control the entire match directly from your wrist.
• Instant Sync: Built on high-performance connectivity. Tapping your watch updates your iPhone in real-time, and vice-versa.
• Wrist Gestures & Tactile Haptics: Tap to score, long-press to undo. Custom vibration signatures let you score without even looking at the screen.

 3. Live Activities & Dynamic Island Integration
• Lock Screen Scoreboard: Lock your phone to save battery. The live score, set count, and active server are projected onto your Lock Screen in real-time.
• Dynamic Island Support: Minimizes into a beautiful, compact status pill while you use other apps. Long-press to open a rich, glassmorphic scoreboard overlay.

4. Premium Vocal Referee & Smart Audio Ducking
• Your Personal Umpire: High-quality vocal referee announces scores and critical highlights ("Match Point!", "Set Point!", "Deuce!").
• Smart Audio Ducking: Automatically lowers background music volume (e.g., Spotify, Apple Music) during announcements and restores it instantly afterward.
• Silent Mode Override: Configured to play announcements even when the phone is on silent, so you never miss a score.

5. Official ITTF Rules Compliance
• Match Customization: Play standard modern 11-point games or classic 21-point formats.
• Set Tracking: Supports Best-of-3 or Best-of-5 sets with glowing visual scoreboard indicators.
• Automatic Serve Rotation: Switches every 2 serves (for 11-point games) or every 5 serves (for 21-point games).
• Smart Deuce Handling: Switches to 1 serve per player at deuce (10-10 or 20-20) until a player leads by 2.
• Manual Override: Tap the "SERVE" indicator at any time to reassign serving rights manually.

6. Exquisite Glowing Cyberpunk Themes
Choose between three professionally curated high-contrast neon palettes:
1. Classic Neon: Hot Pink & Cyan Blue
2. Mint & Royal: Mint Green & Deep Purple
3. Solar Flare: Solar Orange & Teal Green

7. Absolute Privacy & Offline-First
• Zero User Tracking: No registration, no ads, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs.
• 100% Offline: The app requires zero internet access. All data, custom rules, player names, and histories stay entirely on your device.
