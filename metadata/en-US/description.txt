Stop arguing about the score.

Ping Pong Counter tracks points, sets and who serves next on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Tap a player's side to add a point. Swipe down to undo. That is the whole interface — a full-screen neon scoreboard you can read from across the table.

SCORING
• Tap to score, swipe down to undo, with 30 steps of history
• A glowing pulse always marks the player who is serving
• Horizontal layout for a phone propped at the net, vertical for one-handed play
• Match timer, custom player names, and three high-contrast neon themes

DOUBLES AND PLAYERS
• Full doubles support, with automatic partner and serve rotation
• A saved player roster — pull in names with a single tap
• Head-to-head records and win rates for everyone you play

HISTORY AND STATISTICS
• Every match saved automatically, with a set-by-set breakdown and duration
• Win rates, longest matches and average match length
• Optional iCloud sync keeps your history on all your devices
• Export everything as CSV or JSON

ON YOUR WRIST AND LOCK SCREEN
• Native Apple Watch app with instant two-way sync and haptic feedback

VOCAL REFEREE

OFFICIAL RULES
